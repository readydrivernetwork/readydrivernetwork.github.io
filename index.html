Questions

Straight answers

I applied to Uber weeks ago and I'm still not activated. Can you help?
Nine times out of ten that's the vehicle step, not you. The app won't finish approving you until there's a car on file — and that information has to come from the car's side. That's what we send over. Apply or text us and we'll walk you through it.
Is this a job?
No — you work for yourself. You're an independent contractor on whatever apps you choose. We rent you the car and help you get set up; what you earn is yours. When it's slow, we may pass side work your way, but the apps are your main thing.
Do you take a percentage of what I make?
No. You pay a flat weekly rental. We never touch your earnings.
What does it cost?
Sedans run $350–$400/week, SUVs $450/week and the Tahoe $850/week. Maintenance is included and insurance is set up with every rental. Every car and its exact price is on the our cars page, with what’s free right now — the deposit depends on the car, so text us for that.
Is the car insured?
Yes. Every renter is covered before they drive off. We set up the coverage based on how long you plan to rent.
Do you check credit?
No. You need a valid driver's license and a driving record that clears the apps' own background checks. A card deposit holds your car; the amount depends on the car.
My car broke down and I'm losing the week. How fast can I get one?
Text 754-307-0967 now. Most pickups are set up within a day or so, depending on what's ready to go out.
How do I pay?
A deposit by debit or credit card is required to hold your car or schedule a delivery — we don't bring a car out without it. The rest can be paid by card, cash, Zelle, PayPal, Current, Cash App, Apple Pay or Bitcoin. Once your payment is confirmed and the agreement is signed, the car is yours for the week.
Where are you?
We serve Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. Pickup details are set when you text.
