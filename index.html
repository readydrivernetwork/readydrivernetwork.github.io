Questions

Straight answers

I applied to Uber weeks ago and I'm still not activated. Can you help?
Nine times out of ten that's the vehicle step, not you. The app won't finish approving you until there's a car on file — and that information has to come from the car's side. That's what we send over. Apply or text us and we'll walk you through it.
Is this a job?
No — you work for yourself. You're an independent contractor on whatever apps you choose. We rent you the car and help you get set up; what you earn is yours. When it's slow, we may pass side work your way, but the apps are your main thing.
Do you take a percentage of what I make?
No. You pay a flat weekly rental. We never touch your earnings.
What does it cost?
It depends on the vehicle. Insurance and maintenance are included in the weekly rate. Apply or text us and we'll quote your exact rate and deposit for the car that fits you.
Do you check credit?
No. You need a valid driver's license and a driving record that clears the apps' own background checks. The rental payment and deposit are due at pickup, before you drive off.
My car broke down and I'm losing the week. How fast can I get one?
Text 754-307-0967 now. Most pickups are set up within a day or so, depending on what's ready to go out.
How do I pay?
Cash, Zelle, Apple Pay, Cash App, PayPal, debit/credit, or Bitcoin.
Where are you?
We serve Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. Pickup details are set when you text.
